Common questions

What is free, and what does the one-time unlock add?

Free covers the part you need during an attack: recording a spell in 3 taps, the duration timer, triggers, accompanying symptoms, medication, and the full list of everything you have recorded. That is not a trial and it does not expire.

The one-time DizzyLog Pro purchase adds the doctor-ready PDF report and the barometric-pressure charts. It is a single purchase, not a subscription.

I paid but the app still shows the lock

Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. The unlock is tied to the Apple ID, not the device, so it carries over to a new phone.

How do I give the report to my doctor?

Open Report, pick the period, and share the PDF — print it, mail it to yourself, or hand your phone over. It is one page per period: how often, how long, how severe, what you noted as triggers, and what you took.

There is also a CSV export of every raw record, in case you or your clinic want the numbers rather than the summary.

What does the barometric pressure actually mean?

DizzyLog records the air pressure at the moment you log an attack, and the charts show your attacks and the pressure on the same timeline. That is all it does.

It does not tell you that pressure caused an attack, and it does not predict one. Whether weather affects your symptoms is a question for you and your doctor — the app's job is to put the two side by side so the question can be asked with real dates on it.

Where is my data, and does it leave my phone?

Everything you record stays in the app on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Your records are included in your own iPhone or iCloud device backup, which only you control.

The one thing that goes out is a rounded location — used only to look up the air pressure near you, and coarse enough that it does not identify where you are. The details are in the privacy policy, and you can also pick a region by hand and never grant location at all.

Can I use it without giving location access?

Yes. Choose a region manually in Settings, or skip pressure entirely. Recording attacks does not depend on it.

Important

DizzyLog is a journal, not a medical device

It records what you tell it. It does not diagnose, does not predict attacks, and does not give treatment advice.

  • New, sudden or severe dizziness needs a doctor, not an app.
  • Dizziness with chest pain, weakness on one side, trouble speaking, double vision or sudden hearing loss is an emergency — seek care immediately.
  • Never change or stop a medication because of something you saw in a chart here.
