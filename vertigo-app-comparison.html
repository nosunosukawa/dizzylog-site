Which app logs vertigo attacks and exports a PDF for your doctor?
Vertigo tracker apps compared (October 2026)

Checked October 10, 2026, against each app's App Store listing

If you want to log vertigo attacks as they happen and bring one PDF to your ENT, look at apps built for dizziness: Rin Sky: Vertigo Tracker, Meni, Dizzin and Menierly all export a PDF.

They differ in focus. Meni and Menierly are built around Ménière's disease, Dizzin covers vestibular conditions including undiagnosed dizziness, and Rin Sky logs vertigo and headaches side by side with the barometric pressure at each attack. If migraine is your main problem, Migraine Buddy is the established choice.

This page is written by the developer of Rin Sky. To keep it fair, each app is described only by what its own App Store listing says, with nothing added by guesswork. Prices and what sits behind a paywall change, so they are not listed. All of these are journals; none of them diagnoses or treats anything.

Rin Sky: Vertigo TrackerThis site's app

Formerly DizzyLog. The first question is headache, dizziness or both; for dizziness you tap the type (spinning, floating, lightheaded, other), how long it lasted and how bad it was on three steps (mild, moderate, severe). A timer measures the duration, and the iPhone's built-in barometer is read the moment you save. The doctor PDF covers the last 30 days, 90 days or everything, and records also export as CSV. Records stay in a database on the phone; there is no account.

Not for you if: you want it on iPad or Android, or synced across devices (iPhone only, no sync). You want a community, or Apple Health integration (it does not read or write Health).

Source: App Store (id6799015322). Not related to “Rin: Migraine Tracker”, which is a different app from a different developer.

Meniere's: Symptom Tracker (Meni)

Built for Ménière's disease. A live timer runs on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island from the moment an attack starts; when it ends you rate severity, tag symptoms and add notes. A daily log covers sodium, stress, caffeine, alcohol, sleep and water, with a calendar heatmap and trigger correlations. It records barometric pressure and weather, integrates with Apple Health, exports a PDF or CSV for your ENT or audiologist, and offers optional iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad.

Suits you if: you have Ménière's, want iPad as well, or want Apple Health integration.

Source: App Store (id6760962126), by Umut Bozdag

Dizzin: Vertigo & Dizziness

A vertigo and dizziness tracker for Ménière's, vestibular migraine, BPPV, PPPD and vestibular neuritis, and for people still looking for a diagnosis. You rate intensity on a dial and tag the type (spinning, rocking, lightheaded) and what helped. A daily check-in covers sleep, sodium, stress, water, caffeine, medications and cycle; patterns are analyzed on the phone, and a PDF can be exported for your doctor.

Suits you if: your dizziness has no diagnosis yet, or you want pattern summaries written out in plain sentences.

Source: App Store (id6787637461), by BYTESAGE LABS LLP

Menierly: Menieres Toolkit

A self-management toolkit for Ménière's disease. Attack logs include date, time, duration, severity, affected ear, drop attacks, nausea, tinnitus, triggers and medication; daily logs cover stress, sleep, caffeine, ear fullness, tinnitus, hearing changes and balance. It calculates sodium per serving against a daily target, generates a PDF for any date range, exports CSV, and works offline with no account.

Suits you if: you track sodium closely, or want to record which ear and drop attacks.

Source: App Store (id6765802806), by Virtual Integrity Pty Ltd

Migraine Buddy

A long-running migraine and headache tracker with customizable attack logs, exports for doctors, a 7-day weather forecast with pressure, automatic sleep tracking and a large user community. Vertigo is not its main focus.

Suits you if: migraine or headache is the main thing you track, and you want a community.

Source: App Store (id975074413), by Voluntis

How to choose

  • Ménière's, with iPad or Apple Health → Meni.
  • Ménière's, with sodium tracking → Menierly.
  • Dizziness without a diagnosis yet → Dizzin.
  • Vertigo and headaches in one log, with the pressure measured at each attack → Rin Sky.
  • Mainly migraine → Migraine Buddy.

Whichever app you use, the details that help a doctor are the same: how long it lasted, what kind of dizziness, what you were doing, and any ear symptoms. See how to keep a dizziness diary your doctor can use.
